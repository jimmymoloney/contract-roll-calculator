How to use this app

Open in a real browser

Double-click index.html (or open it in Chrome, Edge, or Safari). Upload may not work inside Cursor’s file preview — that is a browser limitation.

Keep the whole docs folder together (including vendor/) so fonts and the spreadsheet reader work offline.

One spreadsheet for everything

Open Data to choose a spreadsheet, load sample data, or clear everything. Upload once; the browser keeps a local copy for Dashboard, Accounts, and Calculator until you clear it.

Clear all data (sidebar, Data, or Settings) wipes deals and settings from this browser.

  • Dates may be month/year only (10/2026) — treated as the 1st of that month.
  • Copy/capital rates may be dollars per page (0.005) or cents (0.5) — auto-detected.
  • Volumes and money may include commas and $.

Settings

Defaults used when a sheet row leaves a field blank, plus payout rules: factor rate, COGs, new-deal service rates, contract term, escalation, extra months on competitor payout, and the “Roll soon” window.

Reset settings to defaults restores factory numbers without clearing deals. Clear all data clears everything.

Dashboard

Date filters, overview totals, month-over-month, year-over-year, deal types, and a roll pipeline that links into Accounts.

Accounts to roll

Runs the correct engine per deal type. Needs-review rows for bad type flags. Filter by Roll now, Roll soon, Build relationship, or All. Click a row to open the calculator.

Roll calculator

Select a customer or enter figures manually. Choose Print Management, Print Advantage, or Rental. See the Calculator guide for type rules.

Roll calculator — three agreement types

Term length, escalation %, and the extra months on competitor payout are editable under Settings (defaults below).

Print Management

  • Capital in click rates (Rental Capital B/W / Colour) + copy costs.
  • Escalation every 6 months from start: 6% on the whole total; extra dollars onto service; capital fixed.
  • Overs (lifetime extras) × capital rates reduce internal payout only. Example: 1000 colour × 9.8¢ = $98.00.
  • Internal = capital monthly × remaining − overs credit.
  • External = current total × (remaining + 1).

Print Advantage

  • Fixed rental + copy costs (e.g. $100.00 + 1000 B/W @ 0.5¢ + 1000 colour @ 5¢ = $55.00 service → $155.00 total).
  • Escalation every 6 months from start: 6% on service only. First uplift: $55.00 × 1.06 = $58.30 → total $158.30 (rental stays $100.00).
  • Overs do not reduce internal payout.
  • Internal = fixed rental × remaining. External = current total × (remaining + 1).

Rental

  • Monthly minimum = fixed rental only. No included volume in the minimum.
  • Copy costs on the sheet are pay-as-you-go and not part of buyout minimum.
  • No service escalation on the minimum.
  • Internal = rental × remaining. External = rental × (remaining + 1).
  • Risk compares competitor replica to rental only (base service floor = 0).

Shared for all types

  • Factor rate, My COG, Competitor COG.
  • Latest safe roll month = last month before competitor replica monthly ≤ current minimum.
  • GP = max principal from monthly room − internal payout − My COG.

Customer quick-select

Populate from the shared spreadsheet (Customer column recommended). Selecting a row sets type and fields; you can still edit afterwards.
