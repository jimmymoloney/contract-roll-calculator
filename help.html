How to use this app

What this tool does

In managed print, customers sit on long contracts. Re-sign (“roll”) too early and the remaining-term payout eats your margin; leave it too late and a competitor can afford to buy them out. This tool finds the best month to re-sign each customer — late enough to protect gross profit, early enough that a competitor still cannot beat your deal. Upload your book once, then use the Dashboard for overview, Accounts to roll for prioritised lists, and the Roll calculator for a month-by-month view of one customer. Everything runs in your browser; customer data never leaves this computer.

Quick start

  1. Open Data (or the empty Dashboard) and choose Load sample data.
  2. Open Accounts to roll to see who is Roll now, Roll soon, or Build relationship.
  3. Click a customer to open them in the Roll calculator and inspect latest safe month, payouts, and GP.

Glossary

  • Roll — Re-signing a customer onto a new contract before their current one ends.
  • Latest safe month — The last month you can still roll before a competitor’s offer would beat the customer’s current minimum.
  • Internal payout — What it costs you to buy out the remaining term (your own payout figure).
  • External payout — What a competitor would typically need to pay to buy the customer out.
  • COG — Cost of goods for the new deal (your cost vs a competitor’s).
  • Factor rate — The financing factor used to turn monthly room into a maximum principal / GP figure.
  • Overs — Lifetime click volume above the included amount; on Print Management they credit the internal payout.
  • Competitor replica — A modelled competitor deal at current volumes and rates, used to judge when they can undercut you.
  • GP — Gross profit available on the roll after internal payout and your COG.

Data

Open Data to upload a spreadsheet, load sample data, or clear everything. Upload once; the browser keeps a local copy for Dashboard, Accounts, and Calculator until you clear it.

Clear all data (sidebar, Data, or Settings) wipes deals and settings from this browser.

  • Dates may be month/year only (10/2026) — treated as the 1st of that month.
  • Copy/capital rates may be dollars per page (0.005) or cents (0.5) — auto-detected.
  • Volumes and money may include commas and $.

Settings

Defaults used when a sheet row leaves a field blank, plus payout rules: factor rate, COGs, new-deal service rates, contract term, escalation, extra months on competitor payout, and the “Roll soon” window.

Reset settings to defaults restores factory numbers without clearing deals. Clear all data clears everything.

Dashboard

Date filters, overview totals, month-over-month, year-over-year, deal types, and a roll pipeline that links into Accounts.

Accounts to roll

Runs the correct engine per deal type. Needs-review rows for bad type flags. Filter by Roll now, Roll soon, Build relationship, or All. Click a row to open the calculator.

Roll calculator

Select a customer or enter figures manually. Choose Print Management, Print Advantage, or Rental. See the Calculator guide for type rules.

Roll calculator — three agreement types

Term length, escalation %, and the extra months on competitor payout are editable under Settings.

Print Management

Capital sits in the click rates (Rental Capital B/W and Colour), plus copy costs.

  • Escalation every 6 months from start: 6% on the whole total; the uplift goes onto service; capital stays fixed.
  • Overs (lifetime extras × capital rates) reduce the internal payout only. Example: 1000 colour × 9.8¢ = $98.00.
  • Internal = capital monthly × months remaining − overs credit.
  • External = current total × (months remaining + 1).

Print Advantage

Fixed rental plus copy costs. Example: $100.00 rental + 1000 B/W @ 0.5¢ + 1000 colour @ 5¢ = $55.00 service → $155.00 total.

  • Escalation every 6 months from start: 6% on service only. First uplift: $55.00 × 1.06 = $58.30 → total $158.30 (rental stays $100.00).
  • Overs do not reduce internal payout.
  • Internal = fixed rental × months remaining. External = current total × (months remaining + 1).

Rental

Monthly minimum is fixed rental only — no included volume in the minimum. Copy costs on the sheet are pay-as-you-go and are not part of the buyout minimum.

  • No service escalation on the minimum.
  • Internal = rental × months remaining. External = rental × (months remaining + 1).
  • Risk compares the competitor replica to rental only (base service floor = 0).

Shared for all types

  • Uses factor rate, My COG, and Competitor COG from Settings (or the row).
  • Latest safe roll month = last month before the competitor replica monthly would be ≤ the current minimum.
  • GP = max principal from monthly room − internal payout − My COG.

Customer quick-select

Pick a customer from the shared spreadsheet (Customer column recommended). Selecting a row sets agreement type and fields; you can still edit afterwards.
