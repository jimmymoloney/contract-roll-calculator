# Contract Roll Calculator — how to use it

## Open in a real browser first

**Double-click `index.html`** (or open it in **Chrome, Edge, or Safari**).  
Upload often does **nothing** inside Cursor’s file preview — that is a browser limitation, not a broken button.

Keep the project folder together (including `vendor/`) so the spreadsheet reader works **offline**.

Menu: **Dashboard | Accounts to roll | Roll calculator | Data | Settings | Help**.

---

## One spreadsheet for everything

Open **Data** (or use **Load sample data** on the empty Dashboard). Upload once; the browser keeps a local copy for all pages until you clear it.

**Clear all data** (sidebar, Data, or Settings) wipes deals **and** settings from this browser.

**Date & rate formats**

- Dates may be month/year only (`10/2026`) — treated as the 1st of that month.
- Copy/capital rates may be dollars per page (`0.005`) or cents (`0.5`) — auto-detected.
- Volumes and money may include commas and `$`.

---

## Settings

Open **Settings** to change defaults used when a sheet row leaves a field blank, and the payout rules:

- Factor rate, My COG, Competitor COG  
- New-deal B/W and colour service rates (¢ per page)  
- Contract term (months), escalation %, escalation every N months  
- Extra months on competitor payout (early termination)  
- “Roll soon” window  

**Reset settings to defaults** restores factory numbers without clearing your deals. **Clear all data** clears everything.

---

## Dashboard

Date filters, overview totals, month-over-month, year-over-year, deal types, and roll pipeline links into Accounts.

---

## Accounts to roll

Runs the correct engine per deal type. Needs-review rows for bad type flags. Filter Roll now / soon / Build relationship / All. Click a row to open the calculator.

---

## Roll calculator

Select a customer or enter manually. Choose Print Management, Print Advantage, or Rental. See the Calculator section in **Help** for type rules.

Demo with fictional data — data never leaves your browser.
