Spreadsheet format

Column names are flexible (capitals ignored). Use Download blank template for a ready-made header row plus three example deals.

  • Customer (recommended — for quick-select)
  • Date of Deal Won, New Devices, Revenue, GP
  • Fixed Rental, B/W Included, Copy Cost B/W, Rental Capital B/W
  • Colour Included, Copy Cost Colour, Rental Capital Colour
  • Minimum monthly Commitment, Total Customer Commitment, New Business
  • Deal Print Management?, Deal Print Advantage?, Deal Rental?
  • Optional: overs, Factor, My COG, Competitor COG

Column guide

Column Required? Example Meaning
Customer Optional Example Co A Account name for lists and quick-select.
Date of Deal Won Required 2024-06-01 When the deal started (or month/year like 6/2024).
New Devices Optional 2 Devices sold on this deal (Dashboard stats).
Revenue Optional 25000 Deal revenue for Dashboard totals.
GP Optional 6000 Gross profit recorded on the original deal.
Fixed Rental Optional 200 Monthly fixed rental (Print Advantage / Rental).
B/W Included Optional 5000 Black-and-white pages included each month.
Copy Cost B/W Optional 1 B/W service rate (¢/page, or $/page if under 0.1).
Rental Capital B/W Optional 10 B/W capital in the click rate (Print Management).
Colour Included Optional 2000 Colour pages included each month.
Copy Cost Colour Optional 6 Colour service rate (¢/page, or $/page if under 0.1).
Rental Capital Colour Optional 12 Colour capital in the click rate (Print Management).
Minimum monthly Commitment Optional 910 Customer’s current monthly minimum ($).
Total Customer Commitment Optional 54600 Full contract value for Dashboard.
New Business Optional Yes Yes/No (or a dollar amount) for new-business reporting.
Deal Print Management? Required* Yes Mark Yes if this is a Print Management deal.
Deal Print Advantage? Required* Yes Mark Yes if this is a Print Advantage deal.
Deal Rental? Required* Yes Mark Yes if this is a Rental deal.
B/W Overs Optional 1000 Lifetime B/W clicks above included (PM payout credit).
Colour Overs Optional 500 Lifetime colour clicks above included (PM payout credit).
Factor Optional 2 Financing factor for this deal (else Settings default).
My COG Optional 6000 Your cost of goods on a new roll ($).
Competitor COG Optional 6000 Assumed competitor cost of goods ($).

* Exactly one of the three deal-type columns should be Yes on each row.

Dates may be month/year only (10/2026). Copy/capital rates may be dollars per page (0.005) or cents (0.5). Volumes and money may include commas and $.
