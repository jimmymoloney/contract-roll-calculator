Spreadsheet format

Column names are flexible (capitals ignored).

  • Customer (recommended — for quick-select)
  • Date of Deal Won, New Devices, Revenue, GP
  • Fixed Rental, B/W Included, Copy Cost B/W, Rental Capital B/W
  • Colour Included, Copy Cost Colour, Rental Capital Colour
  • Minimum monthly Commitment, Total Customer Commitment, New Business
  • Deal Print Management?, Deal Print Advantage?, Deal Rental?
  • Optional: overs, Factor, My COG, Competitor COG

Dates may be month/year only (10/2026). Copy/capital rates may be dollars per page (0.005) or cents (0.5). Volumes and money may include commas and $.
